## Coverage gaps

These are limits of the admitted registry, not missing scientific values. A country or science absent here is not silently filled from another source.

- Wildfire perimeters: the admitted providers cover the United States (NIFC WFIGS) and Canada (CWFIS) only. No admitted provider covers another country's current perimeters.
- Warnings: the admitted provider covers the United States and its territories (National Weather Service). MeteoAlarm is not admitted because its licence has not been verified, so no European warning feed is recorded.
- National radar archives: the admitted archive is NEXRAD for the United States. RainViewer is a global composite, not a national archive. DWD, ECCC GeoMet and the Australian Bureau of Meteorology are not admitted because their licences have not been verified.
- Volcano notices: the admitted provider covers United States volcanoes and territories (USGS). The Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program is not admitted because its licence has not been verified, so no global volcano feed is recorded.
- River gauges: real-time gauge observations are admitted for the United States (USGS) and England (Environment Agency). GloFAS is a global model discharge, not a gauge observation; no other national gauge network is recorded.
- Terrain: the admitted provider gives a Copernicus DEM GLO-90 height for land points. It does not state a vertical datum, and no admitted provider supplies terrain for the whole ocean floor.
